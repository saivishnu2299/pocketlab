# Code of conduct

PocketLAB is a small hobby project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests or discussions, is expected to help keep it friendly and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the Brick, to C or to coding agents.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone else's personal information, and remove your own serial numbers, paths and saved data from logs.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexual content or unwelcome attention.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Repeated off-topic posting or spam.
- Sharing or requesting pirated ROMs, firmware images or other material you have no right to distribute.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem with GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request, choosing to report it to the repository maintainers. For anything that must stay private, use the private reporting form described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and start the title with "Conduct".

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, or block people who break this code. Reports are handled confidentially.
